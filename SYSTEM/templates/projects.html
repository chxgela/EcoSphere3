Port of Batangas — Greening Project

A sustainability initiative focused on reducing emissions, improving energy efficiency, and strengthening environmental management systems in port operations.

Location: Batangas, Philippines
Duration: Ongoing
Impact:

  • Reduced energy consumption and emissions
  • Strengthened environmental monitoring practices
Goals:
  • Mitigate climate change impacts

  • Enhance sustainable port operations

SDG 15

First Gen Corporation — Batangas Eco-Park & Wildlife Rescue Center

A rehabilitation project transforming a former dumpsite into a sustainable eco-park and wildlife rescue center, promoting biodiversity conservation and environmental restoration.

Location: Batangas City, Philippines
Duration: Ongoing
Impact:
  • Conversion of a 2-hectare closed dumpsite into an eco-park
  • Development of a wildlife rescue and rehabilitation center
  • Creation of habitats for rescued animals (aviary, serpentarium, turtle pond)
  • Establishment of recreational facilities for the public
  • Tree planting of endemic hardwood species
  • Support for biodiversity conservation and habitat restoration
Goals:
  • Restore degraded land and ecosystems
  • Promote biodiversity and wildlife protection
  • Transform waste sites into sustainable green spaces
  • Encourage environmental awareness and eco-tourism
  • Support climate-resilient urban development
